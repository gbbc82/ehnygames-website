01 / THE STORY

이야기

데굴데굴, 친구를 구하러!

볼볼이와 함께 여섯 나라를 굴러다니며, 투덜 대왕에게 잡혀간 친구들을 구해 주세요.

02 / HOW TO PLAY

이런 게임이에요

⭐120개 스테이지

월드마다 20개 스테이지. 숨은 별을 찾고, 열쇠를 모아 친구를 구해요.

🪽굴리기, 점프, 날개

화면을 끌어 굴리고, 빠르게 밀면 부스트! 점프 버튼으로 장애물을 넘고, 날개로 다음 체크포인트까지 날아가요.

🎚️내게 맞는 난이도

쉬움부터 엄청 어려움까지 5단계. 쉬움에서는 장애물이 줄고 체크포인트가 늘어나요.

공 33종과 꼬리 효과 10종으로 꾸며 보세요.

BolBol Land

3D rolling adventure · A journey through six worlds for kids and grown-ups

01 / THE STORY

The Story

Roll out to rescue your friends!

Roll through six worlds with BolBol and rescue the friends captured by King Grumble.

02 / HOW TO PLAY

What You'll Do

⭐120 stages

Explore 20 stages per world, find hidden stars and collect keys to rescue friends.

🪽Roll, jump and glide

Drag to roll, swipe quickly to boost, jump over obstacles and use wings to fly to the next checkpoint.

🎚️Play at your own pace

Choose from five difficulty levels. Easy mode has fewer obstacles and more checkpoints.

Customize your look with 33 balls and 10 trail effects.

Explore the six worlds and meet your friends

Journey through six worlds

WORLD 01

Toy Factory

Ride conveyors and trampolines to rescue Bunhongi.

WORLD 02

Green Forest

Ride a leaf and cross log bridges to rescue Darami.

WORLD 03

Playground

Pass swings and slides to rescue Quacky.

WORLD 04

Dessert Land

Cross straw paths and cookie bridges to rescue Pudding.

WORLD 05

Amusement Park

Roll past the Ferris wheel and roller coaster to rescue Popo.

WORLD 06

Grumble Castle

Cross the drawbridge, escape King Grumble and rescue Princess Bormi.

Meet the characters

With a rainbow hood, a shiny star and a red cape, BolBol curls into a ball and rolls off to rescue friends.
