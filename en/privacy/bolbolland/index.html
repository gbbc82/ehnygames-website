BolBol Land Privacy Policy

Pre-release notice

This notice describes confirmed practices and items under review for EHNY Games’ Android game BolBol Land. The policy and its effective date will be finalized after review of the release build.

Information and purposes

The game has no registration or login. Game progress is stored on the device so players can resume play. The current game has no ads or advertising SDKs.

Unity and Android services

Unity Analytics and Cloud Diagnostics are disabled. Work is underway to disable Unity Engine Diagnostics and hardware statistics and to change Android Auto Backup settings. After checking diagnostic transmissions and the scope of backup and restoration in the release build, we will identify the data types, purposes and recipients where applicable.

In-app purchases

In-app purchases are currently unimplemented. A one-time US$2.99 purchase to unlock Worlds 3–6 is planned for release. This policy will be updated with the applicable data practices and third-party services after Google Play Billing is implemented and reviewed.

Retention and deletion

You can delete locally stored progress by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings. This resets progress on that device. Retention, restoration and deletion of any separate backup copies will be addressed after the final backup settings are reviewed. If you email us, we use your address and message to respond and follow up. Contact us below to request deletion of your correspondence.

Children’s privacy

BolBol Land is intended for players including children. Parents and guardians can ask about their child’s personal information or request deletion. Please include only the information needed for us to respond.
