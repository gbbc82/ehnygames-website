BolBol Land Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

EHNY Games (“we”) cares about the privacy of the children and parents who use the BolBol Land app (“the app”).

1. Information we collect

  • The app does not collect personal information. This includes names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, photos, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID.
  • There is no sign-up or login.
  • We do not run any servers, and the app does not send any information to us.
  • The only outside communication the app makes is with Google Play, when you buy or restore a purchase.

2. Information stored on your device and backup

  • To save game progress, the app stores the following on your device:
    • stage progress, coins, selected balls and trails, race records, and whether you own the Full Version
    • sound, music, language and difficulty settings
  • This information is never sent to us.
  • If Google backup is turned on for the device, this save file may be backed up to your Google account by Android’s built-in backup feature.
    • The backup lets your progress come back when you reinstall the app or move to a new device.
    • Google manages this backup, and we cannot see or receive it.
  • To start over, use “Clear data” for the app in your device settings.

3. In-app purchase

  • Worlds 1–2 are free. Worlds 3–6 can be unlocked with a one-time Full Version purchase.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing.
    • Payment details (such as card numbers) are processed by Google, and we never receive them.
  • The app only checks with Google Play whether the Full Version was purchased. The result is stored only on the device and is never sent to us.
  • If you reinstall the app, the Full Version is restored from the purchase history of the same Google account.
  • The purchase button is behind a parent check (a multiplication question).
  • Refunds follow Google Play’s refund policy.

4. Ads, analytics and third parties

  • The app contains no ads.
  • The app contains no analytics tools.
  • We do not share or sell personal information.

5. Permissions

  • The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access.
  • The app’s details may list these system permissions:
    • Google Play billing
    • network state, foreground service, boot completed, and wake lock
  • They come from Google Play Billing and from Google Play Asset Delivery, which downloads the game’s data. They are not used to collect personal information.

6. Google Play

  • The app is distributed through Google Play.
  • Information that Google handles for installation, updates, payments and Android backup is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. Children

  • The app is made for children aged 6–12.
  • We do not collect personal information from children.
  • The purchase button and external links, such as this privacy policy, are behind a parent check.

8. Changes

  • If the information the app handles changes, we will update this page before the change takes effect.
  • This page will show the change and its effective date.

9. Contact

  • Developer: EHNY Games
  • Privacy questions: privacy@ehnygames.com
  • Other questions: support@ehnygames.com
