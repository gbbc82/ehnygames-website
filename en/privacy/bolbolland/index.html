BolBol Land Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Operated by EHNY Games · Privacy contact: privacy@ehnygames.com

EHNY Games ("we") makes BolBol Land (the "app"), a game for children aged 6–12. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Personal information we collect

The app does not send personal information to us, and we do not collect or store users' personal information.

  • There is no sign-up or log-in. The app does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos or location.
  • There are no ads, no advertising ID, and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.

2. Game data stored only on the device

Game progress (cleared stages, stars, coins, race records), unlocked balls and decorations, whether the full game was purchased, and settings (language, sound, difficulty) are stored only on the user's device and are not sent to us.

3. Android Auto Backup

So that progress is kept after reinstalling, only the game save file is included in Android Auto Backup. The backup is stored in the user's own Google account only if Google backup is turned on for the device, and is handled under Google's policies. We cannot access it. It can be turned off or deleted in the device or Google account settings.

4. In-app purchase (Google Play billing)

Worlds 3–6 and the ending unlock with a single purchase (full game). The purchase screen opens only after a parent check.

  • Payments are processed only through Google Play's billing system. Payment details such as card numbers are collected directly by Google; neither the app nor we receive or see them.
  • To unlock the full game, the app receives the purchase status (product ID, whether the purchase is complete, and purchase verification information) from Google Play on the device. This information is not sent to our servers.
  • To process purchases, the Google Play Billing Library (provided by Google) included in the app communicates with Google Play over the internet. Purchase and diagnostic information may be processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Through Google Play Console we can see sales records provided by Google (such as order number, country and amount), used only for settlement and refunds.
  • Playing the game itself does not require the internet.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not share or sell users' personal information. Payments are handled by Google under its own policies, as described in section 4.

6. Children's privacy

The app is made for children and does not collect personal information from children. Features that need a parent's decision, such as purchases, require a parent check. We aim to comply with applicable laws including Korea's Personal Information Protection Act, the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Deleting data

  • Game data on the device is deleted when the app is uninstalled.
  • Backups can be deleted as described in section 3.
  • Purchase records are managed by Google Play. For refunds or purchase questions, contact Google Play or us below.

8. Rights of users and parents

Users and their parents or guardians may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of personal information. Because we do not store personal information, most requests can be handled directly as described in section 7. We will respond promptly to any inquiry.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page with a new effective date, and will also mention significant changes in app update notes.

10. Contact

  • Privacy: privacy@ehnygames.com
  • General: support@ehnygames.com
  • Website: https://ehnygames.com
